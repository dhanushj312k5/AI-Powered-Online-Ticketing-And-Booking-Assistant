
# Team Presentation Responsibilities

| Member | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| Member 1 | Introduction, problem statement and conclusion |
| Member 2 | Proposed solution and key features |
| Member 3 | AI capabilities and system architecture |
| Member 4 | Technology stack and implementation |
| Demo Lead | Live application demonstration |

## Presentation Guidelines

- Each member must understand their assigned slides.
- Each member must explain their contribution.
- Avoid reading directly from slides.
- Keep the demonstration ready before presenting.
- Prepare answers for technical questions.
