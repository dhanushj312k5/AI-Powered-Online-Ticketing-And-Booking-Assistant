# Slide 2 – Problem Statement

Traditional ticket booking systems have several challenges:

- Complicated booking procedures.
- Difficulty finding suitable tickets.
- Time-consuming seat selection.
- Confusion regarding ticket pricing.
- Payment failures and booking uncertainty.
- Poor experience for users with limited connectivity.

---

# Slide 3 – Proposed Solution

Our proposed system provides an AI-powered booking assistant.

Key objectives:

- Understand user booking requests.
- Help users discover available tickets.
- Support seat selection.
- Calculate ticket prices automatically.
- Guide users through checkout.
- Provide booking confirmation and QR tickets.
